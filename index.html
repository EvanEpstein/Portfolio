About me

Hello! I'm Evan Epstein, a computer science student with a passion for baseball, soccer, and computer programming. Over the past three years I've taken every level of CS my school offers. I believe that coding helped to develop my problem solving and logical thinking better and the thought process I learned have been invaluable beyond just computers.

I've worked on projects ranging from game clones to Arduino hardware hacks to teaching coding to elementary schoolers. This portfolio captures the ones I'm most proud of. Hope you enjoy exploring it as much as I enjoyed building it.
